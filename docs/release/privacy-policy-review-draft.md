## 1. Information Gallurio collects

Gallurio may collect account identity and contact information, including name,
email address, authentication identifiers, business name, workspace details,
preferences, and support correspondence.

Users may store business and portfolio information, images, galleries, branding,
booking details, client information, inquiries, team membership, and content
they choose to publish publicly.

Gallurio may collect technical and usage information such as IP address,
device/browser information, timestamps, pages viewed, actions taken, security
events, and diagnostic logs.

Gallurio records billing and entitlement information needed to operate Pro,
including plan state, subscription status, provider identifiers, period dates,
expiry/lapse timestamps, and promo redemption history. Lemon Squeezy processes
payment details as Gallurio's Merchant of Record; Gallurio does not directly
store full payment-card details.

## 2. Beta participation and promo eligibility

For the beta program, Gallurio may record that a user identity participated in
the beta and may record the date or source of that eligibility. This record is
used to prevent unauthorized or repeated redemption of the beta participant
benefit.

The beta participant benefit is one two-month Gallurio Pro promo grant. Gallurio
may record redemption, grant start, grant expiry, and redemption status. The
beta program may close globally, ending active beta access, while participant
eligibility and stored product data remain available according to the approved
retention policy.

## 3. How information is used

Gallurio uses information to provide and secure the service, authenticate users,
manage workspaces and memberships, publish portfolio pages, process inquiries,
operate bookings and client records, provide support, process billing events,
enforce entitlements, administer beta/promo eligibility, prevent abuse, improve
the product, and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Service providers and sharing

Gallurio may share information with service providers needed to operate the
service, such as hosting/infrastructure, database, authentication, email,
media/storage, analytics, abuse-prevention, and payment providers. The final
published version must name the actual production providers and link to their
applicable privacy terms.

Gallurio may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or
safety, prevent fraud or abuse, or complete a merger, acquisition, financing,
or sale of assets. Gallurio does not sell personal information.

## 5. Public content

Content published on a Gallurio public page may be visible to anyone with
access to the page or its URL. Users are responsible for ensuring that they do
not publish private or sensitive information unintentionally.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Gallurio retains information as needed to provide the service, administer
billing and beta eligibility, comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent abuse,
and maintain business records. When a paid subscription expires, Gallurio may
unpublish the public page after the approved lifecycle period while retaining
the workspace data so it can be restored after resubscription.

Users may request account or data deletion through `[support/contact route]`.
Requests remain subject to legal, security, backup, dispute, and operational
retention requirements.

## 7. International processing and security

Information may be processed in countries where Gallurio or its providers
operate. Gallurio uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but
no system can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their credentials
and report suspected compromise.

## 8. Rights, cookies, children, and changes

The final policy must state applicable access, correction, deletion, objection,
portability, and complaint rights; cookie controls; the service's age policy;
and how material policy changes are communicated.
