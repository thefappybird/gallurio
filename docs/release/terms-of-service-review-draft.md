## 1. Service

Gallurio provides workspace tools for event and creative businesses, including
bookings, clients, inquiries, teams, galleries, portfolio pages, and related
services. Features may change, be limited, suspended, or removed as the service
develops.

## 2. Accounts and workspaces

Users must provide accurate information, protect their credentials, and remain
responsible for activity under their account. Workspace owners are responsible
for their content, team access, client information, and permissions.

## 3. Beta access

Gallurio's initial beta will run as a two-month program. Beta access is
program-wide and may end for all beta users when Gallurio closes the program;
it is not a guaranteed individual subscription term. Beta features may be
unfinished, changed, limited, suspended, or removed.

Closing beta does not delete user accounts, workspaces, portfolio content,
bookings, clients, galleries, teams, or other stored data.

Verified beta participants may receive one two-month Gallurio Pro promo. The
promo is redeemable once per eligible user identity and eligibility is checked
against Gallurio's beta-participant records before redemption. The promo has no
ordinary redemption deadline; emergency revocation for abuse or compromise
must be handled through an explicit support/operator process. Treatment of an
already-active paid subscription must be stated consistently across the
product and support materials.

## 4. User content and public pages

Users retain responsibility for content they upload or publish and must have
the rights and permissions needed to use it. Public portfolio pages may be
accessible to visitors. Users must not publish unlawful, infringing, abusive,
misleading, or confidential material without authorization.

## 5. Pro subscriptions

Gallurio Pro is offered through monthly and yearly Lemon Squeezy variants only.
There is no Starter product or customer-selected downgrade plan. Prices,
currency, taxes, billing period, and checkout terms are shown before payment
and are authoritative as displayed by the live Lemon Squeezy configuration.

Lemon Squeezy is Gallurio's Merchant of Record and may process payments, taxes,
invoices, billing support, and eligible refunds under its terms and applicable
law.

## 6. Cancellation, failed payment, and expiry

Users may cancel through the available billing portal. Cancellation normally
stops future renewal and does not immediately remove access during the paid
period.

Gallurio follows Lemon Squeezy's payment status. A transient payment failure,
past-due status, or pause does not by itself determine that access has ended.
Gallurio limits access when Lemon Squeezy reports a terminal non-payment,
expiry, refund, or other provider-authoritative terminal event.

After expiry, access is routed to subscription recovery. Gallurio retains the
workspace data. A published public page may be unpublished after the approved
retention period; resubscribing can restore access and permit republishing.

## 7. Refunds

Refunds and payment disputes are handled under the applicable Refund Policy,
Lemon Squeezy's process, and applicable consumer-protection law. Cancellation
does not automatically create a refund for prior charges or unused time unless
required by law or approved under the applicable process.

## 8. Acceptable use, suspension, and liability

Users must not misuse the service, bypass access controls, interfere with
security, infringe rights, or use Gallurio for unlawful activity. Gallurio may
suspend or restrict access for security, abuse, non-payment, legal, or
operational reasons, subject to applicable law. The final draft must include
the approved warranty, liability, indemnity, dispute, governing-law, and
termination language for the operating jurisdiction.
