# Gallurio core-app YouTube demo script

An editable filming script for a 9–12 minute Gallurio walkthrough. This video covers the operational workspace only: inquiries, bookings, clients, teams, reporting, and settings. Link to the separate Portfolio Maker video when you reach the public-site connection.

## Before recording

- Use a dedicated demo workspace with fictional names, email addresses, phone numbers, venues, and payments. Do not show a real client, notification, API key, browser autofill, or account email.
- Seed a small, coherent story: one new wedding inquiry; several upcoming and completed bookings; a few payments; two teams; and a published portfolio that points to the inquiry.
- Record at desktop width with the sidebar expanded. Open each destination in advance and use a clean browser profile.
- Keep the narration in your own voice. Text in quotation marks is a starting point, not wording that must be read exactly.

## Story used throughout

`[Studio name]` receives an inquiry from `[Client name]` for a `[event type]` on `[event date]`. The goal is to show how Gallurio helps an event business move from that first request to a scheduled, paid job without losing the client context.

## Optional inserts

Use only the inserts that are prepared in your demo workspace; do not manufacture a live-looking payment or send real email during filming.

- Import/export: briefly show the bookings toolbar to establish that existing booking records can be brought in or exported.
- Notifications: show the notification area if there is a safe, fake notification that illustrates a new inquiry or team assignment.
- Mobile: capture one short separate shot of the bookings cards or calendar at 375 px. Present it as a responsive view, not as a second full walkthrough.
- Localization: show the language control or Arabic RTL only if every screen you visit is ready; otherwise keep the whole recording in one locale.

## Edit notes

- Prefer cursor-led actions and short pauses after a screen change. Cut loading states, long typing, and repeated table filtering.
- Add chapter cards after the final edit: `Dashboard`, `Inquiries`, `Bookings`, `Clients`, `Teams`, `Business overview`, and `Portfolio connection`.
- Do not promise automations, integrations, payment availability, or outcomes that are not visible in the recording. Say “can help you manage” rather than “guarantees.”
- Replace every bracketed placeholder before publishing.
