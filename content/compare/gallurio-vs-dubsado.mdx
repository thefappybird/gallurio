## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if your real workflow is a personal reply followed by a clear booking,
client, calendar, and invoice process rather than a chain of automated contracts and emails.

Dubsado is built around workflows. A lead fills in a form, and Dubsado can fire a contract, an
invoice, and a timed sequence of emails without you touching it. That machinery is what the price
buys, and for a business that has designed its process around it, the machinery is the product.

Gallurio does not do that. It provides configurable Home and Gallery canvases for story, services,
video, featured projects, and gallery collections, then keeps the resulting inquiries, bookings,
clients, calendar, and invoices in one place. It is <GallurioPrice />.

The honest split: if you have built an automated client journey, Dubsado is worth its price and
Gallurio will feel bare. If your "workflow" is that you reply to the enquiry yourself, you are
paying Dubsado for an engine you never start.

## What Dubsado does that Gallurio does not

- **Chained workflows.** The core of the product. Form submission triggers contract, invoice,
  emails, tasks, on a schedule you define. Gallurio has no equivalent and no partial version of it.
- **Contracts and e-signatures.** Gallurio has none.
- **Proposals and lead capture forms** with conditional logic. Gallurio's public form is a single
  fixed enquiry form.
- **Time tracking and scheduling** for businesses that bill hourly.
- **Client portals** where clients see their own documents.

## What Gallurio does that Dubsado does not

- **A creative storefront you actually design.** The Home and Gallery canvases can combine story,
  services, video, featured projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections using
  composable layouts, brand colours, and typography. Dubsado gives you forms and a client portal,
  not that public showcase.
- **A calendar built around bookings**, not around appointments-as-meetings.
- **Team workspaces** without a tier change.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate.** Gallurio bills the applicable USD tier and shows a
  converted headline when a rate is available. Dubsado is USD only.
- **Five interface languages**, Arabic included with right-to-left layout.

## Portfolio SEO versus workflow automation

Dubsado's value begins after a lead reaches the system. Gallurio also gives that lead somewhere to
discover the business first: published Home and Gallery pages with configurable search titles and
descriptions, canonical URLs, social previews, and structured business/gallery data.

Those settings make the portfolio easier for search engines and AI systems to interpret. They do
not guarantee indexing, ranking, or demand. Owners still need to describe real services and
locations, show specific projects, and write for the questions potential clients actually search.
Choose Gallurio
when that public storefront and the connected booking record matter more than an automated client
journey; choose Dubsado when the automation is the product.

## Who this is not for

Skip Gallurio if:

- Your client journey is automated and you would have to rebuild it by hand.
- Contracts and signatures happen inside your CRM today.
- You bill hourly and need time tracking.
- You have hundreds of active projects to migrate and no appetite for re-entry.
