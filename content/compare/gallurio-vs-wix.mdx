## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if you want two configurable showcase canvases for your brand, skills,
services, projects, and collections, with the booking workflow built directly behind them.

Wix is the broader general-purpose website platform. It has a drag-anything canvas, ecommerce,
blogging, hundreds of templates, and an app market. Gallurio focuses its design freedom on the Home
and Gallery canvases where a creative business presents its skills and strongest work.

Those canvases can combine hero sections, story, services, video, calls to action, featured
projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections. Brand colours, typography, spacing,
imagery, containers, and columns make the result a creative storefront rather than a booking form
with decoration around it.

The comparison is about the other half of the job. A Wix contact form sends you an email. What
turns that email into a booking, a client record, a deposit and an invoice is a second system,
usually a spreadsheet.

Gallurio's public form creates the inquiry, the client and the booking in one transaction, on the
calendar you already work from. Site and system are the same product, for <GallurioPrice />.

## What Wix does that Gallurio does not

- **Absolute positioning and a broader site architecture.** Position elements freely across many
  page types, not only the two portfolio showcase canvases Gallurio provides.
- **A store, a blog, unlimited pages, member areas.**
- **An app market** with hundreds of integrations.
- **Full domain and SEO control** at a level Gallurio does not attempt.
- **Wix Bookings**, if appointment scheduling is what you actually need.

## What Gallurio does that Wix does not

- **A creative storefront connected to the business.** Home and Gallery are independent visual
  canvases for story, services, video, featured projects, and collection-led galleries.
- **Composable layouts and brand control.** Presets or manual containers, columns, images, text,
  buttons, spacing, colours, and typography can be combined around the work.
- **Turns an enquiry into a booking** — one submission, three records, one transaction.
- **A bookings calendar** with statuses and deposits, not an appointments add-on.
- **Invoice PDFs** generated from the booking.
- **Client history** — previous bookings and what was paid.
- **Team access** to one workspace with roles.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate.** Gallurio bills the applicable USD tier and shows a
  converted headline when available; Wix bills in USD.

## How do the SEO capabilities compare?

Wix offers the broader toolbox: many page types, marketing integrations, redirects, apps, and
general-purpose SEO controls. Gallurio focuses on a creative portfolio's technical foundation.
Published Home and Gallery pages are server-rendered and carry configurable search titles and
descriptions, canonical URLs, social preview settings, structured business/gallery data, and an
indexing preference.

That makes an index-enabled portfolio easier for search engines and AI retrieval systems to
understand, but it does not make generic work rank. The owner still needs useful service and
location copy, project evidence, and a page worth recommending. Choose Wix when the public site is
a broad marketing platform; choose Gallurio when discovery should lead directly into an event
booking workspace.

## Who this is not for

Stay on Wix if:

- You sell products online.
- You need content types beyond the Home and Gallery showcase canvases.
- Absolute positioning, ecommerce checkout, blogging, or the app market is central to the site.
- Wix Bookings already covers your scheduling and you are happy with it.

The middle path is legitimate: keep the Wix site, run Gallurio behind it.
