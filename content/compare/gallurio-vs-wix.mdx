## The short version

Wix is a better website builder than Gallurio. It has a drag-anything canvas, a store, a blog,
hundreds of templates and an app market. Nothing here argues otherwise.

The comparison is about the other half of the job. A Wix contact form sends you an email. What
turns that email into a booking, a client record, a deposit and an invoice is a second system,
usually a spreadsheet.

Gallurio's public form creates the inquiry, the client and the booking in one transaction, on the
calendar you already work from. Site and system are the same product, for <GallurioPrice />.

If a website is genuinely all you need, Wix Light at $17 is reasonable and worth keeping.

## What Wix does that Gallurio does not

- **An unconstrained design canvas.** Position anything anywhere. Gallurio gives you a fixed block
  library across three pages.
- **A store, a blog, unlimited pages, member areas.**
- **An app market** with hundreds of integrations.
- **Full domain and SEO control** at a level Gallurio does not attempt.
- **Wix Bookings**, if appointment scheduling is what you actually need.

## What Gallurio does that Wix does not

- **Turns an enquiry into a booking** — one submission, three records, one transaction.
- **A bookings calendar** with statuses and deposits, not an appointments add-on.
- **Invoice PDFs** generated from the booking.
- **Client history** — previous bookings and what was paid.
- **Team access** to one workspace with roles.
- **Local-currency pricing.** Billed in PHP and shown converted to yours; Wix bills in USD.

## Who this is not for

Stay on Wix if:

- You sell products online.
- Your site is more than a portfolio and a contact page.
- Pixel-level design control is part of what clients hire you for.
- Wix Bookings already covers your scheduling and you are happy with it.

The middle path is legitimate: keep the Wix site, run Gallurio behind it.
