## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if you want two configurable showcase canvases for your brand, skills,
services, projects, and collections, with every inquiry turning directly into a client, event
booking, calendar entry, and invoice-ready record.

Squarespace and Gallurio make different website trade-offs. Squarespace supports a broader site
architecture with blogs, stores, memberships, and many page types. Gallurio concentrates its design
freedom on the two pages a creative business uses to sell its capabilities: Home and Gallery.

Those two canvases can combine hero sections, story, services, video, calls to action, featured
projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections. Brand colours, typography, spacing,
imagery, containers, and columns make the result a creative storefront rather than a booking form
with decoration around it.

The question is what happens after a visitor fills in your contact form.

On Squarespace, the answer is: you get an email. Everything after that — the client record, the
booking, the deposit, the calendar, the invoice — lives somewhere else, usually a spreadsheet and
an inbox.

On Gallurio, the form submission creates the client and the booking in the same transaction, on the
same calendar you already work from. The site and the system behind it are one product, for

If you genuinely only need a website, Squarespace at $16 is good value and this page is not trying
to talk you out of it.

## What Squarespace does that Gallurio does not

- **A broader publishing and commerce architecture.** Blog, ecommerce checkout, many page types,
  member areas, and hundreds of templates.
- **Full custom domain control** and mature SEO tooling.
- **An enormous template and plugin ecosystem.**

## What Gallurio does that Squarespace does not

- **A creative storefront connected to the business.** Home and Gallery are independent visual
  canvases for story, services, video, featured projects, and collection-led galleries.
- **Composable layouts and brand control.** Presets or manual containers, columns, images, text,
  buttons, spacing, colours, and typography can be combined around the work.
- **Turns an enquiry into a booking.** One submission writes the inquiry, the client and the
  booking together.
- **A calendar of actual bookings**, with deposits and statuses, not an appointments add-on.
- **Invoice PDFs** generated from the booking.
- **Client history** — what someone booked before, what they paid.
- **Team access** to the same workspace.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate.** Gallurio bills the applicable USD tier and shows a
  converted headline when available; Squarespace bills in USD.

## Who this is not for

Stay on Squarespace if:

- You need content types beyond the Home and Gallery showcase canvases.
- You need ecommerce checkout, memberships, or a substantial blog.
- Your existing Squarespace publishing workflow is already central to the business.

A reasonable middle path exists and is worth naming: keep Squarespace as your public site, and use
Gallurio behind it for bookings, clients and invoices. Nothing about the two prevents that.
