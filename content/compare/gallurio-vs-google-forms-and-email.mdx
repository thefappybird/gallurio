## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if you want every public inquiry to become a tracked client and booking
without copying data between a form, an inbox, a spreadsheet, and a calendar.

The most widely used booking system in the event industry is not HoneyBook or Dubsado. It is a
Google Form, a Gmail inbox, and a spreadsheet. It is free, it is understood by everyone, and for a
lot of businesses it works.

It leaks in one specific place: **the handoff.** The form produces a row and an email. Neither of
those is a booking. A person has to read the email, decide it is real, copy the details into the
spreadsheet, add it to a calendar, and remember to reply. Every one of those steps is a place an
enquiry stops.

Gallurio removes the handoff. The public form writes the inquiry, the client and the booking in a
single transaction — either all three exist or none do. It is <GallurioPrice />.

## What the flow looks like

**With Google Forms and email**

1. Visitor submits the form.
2. A row appears in a spreadsheet.
3. An email arrives in an inbox that also contains everything else.
4. Someone reads it, eventually.
5. Someone copies the details into the bookings sheet.
6. Someone adds it to a calendar.
7. Someone replies.

Steps 4 through 7 are manual, and each is optional in practice.

## What the free stack does that Gallurio does not

- **It costs nothing.**
- **Custom questions, any shape.** Google Forms has conditional logic, file uploads, any field you
  want. Gallurio's contact form is fixed.
- **It plugs into everything** — Sheets, Apps Script, Zapier, whatever you have already wired.
- **Everyone knows how it works**, including whoever covers for you.

If your enquiry form asks fifteen conditional questions, Google Forms is genuinely better at that
part and Gallurio does not try to compete with it.

## What Gallurio does that the free stack does not

- **One transaction, no transcription.** The single largest difference.
- **A configurable creative storefront** — independent Home and Gallery canvases for story,
  services, video, featured projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections — instead
  of a bare form.
- **A calendar of real bookings** with statuses and deposits.
- **Invoice PDFs** generated from the booking.
- **Client history**, kept without maintenance.
- **Team access** with roles, rather than a shared login and a shared sheet.

## Discovery before the form submission

Google Forms solves intake after someone receives the link. It does not create the public evidence
that helps a new client find and trust the business. Gallurio puts the form behind configurable
Home and Gallery pages where the owner can explain services, name the locations served, and prove
the work through real projects.

Published portfolios support configurable search titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, social
previews, structured business/gallery data, and an indexing choice. This gives crawlers and AI
retrieval systems useful context before the inquiry happens. It does not guarantee visibility; the
portfolio still needs specific, credible content and links from places potential clients already
use.

## Who this is not for

Stay with the free stack if:

- Your enquiry form needs conditional logic or file uploads.
- You get few enough enquiries that none get lost.
- You have already automated the handoff with Apps Script or Zapier and it works.
- You are the only person involved and the inbox is genuinely under control.
