## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if unlimited bookings, team access, invoice PDFs, and a public portfolio on
one regional plan matter more than contracts, questionnaires, and automated job stages.

Studio Ninja is a photographer's CRM: jobs, quotes, contracts, questionnaires, invoices, and a
workflow to move a shoot from enquiry to delivered. It is tiered from about **$16 to $40 per
month**, and the entry tier's five-active-job cap means most working photographers pay for Pro or
Master.

Gallurio is one plan, <GallurioPrice />, with no job cap and no tier above it. It provides
configurable Home and Gallery canvases for services, video, featured projects, and gallery
collections, then runs the bookings, clients, calendar, and invoices behind them. It does not do
contracts or questionnaires.

## What Studio Ninja does that Gallurio does not

- **Contracts and e-signatures**, built into the job flow.
- **Client questionnaires**, which for wedding photographers do real work.
- **Job workflows** with stages and automated reminders.
- **Payment processing** through connected gateways.
- **Photographer-specific defaults** — shoot types, deliverable tracking, second shooters.

## What Gallurio does that Studio Ninja does not

- **A configurable creative storefront.** Independent Home and Gallery canvases can combine story,
  services, video, featured projects, and collection-led grid or masonry layouts using your own
  colours, typography, and composition. Studio Ninja gives you booking forms, not that showcase.
- **Unlimited jobs and team members on the entry plan.**
- **Multiple business types in one workspace** — useful if you shoot and also plan or coordinate.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate**, billed in USD and shown converted when available.
- **Five interface languages**, including right-to-left Arabic.

## Which one helps a photographer get discovered?

Studio Ninja is strongest once the photography job exists. Gallurio adds a public discovery layer:
Home can explain the photographer's services and positioning, while Gallery can prove the work
through projects and collections. Owners can configure search-result titles and descriptions,
social preview media, and indexing preferences; the published pages also expose canonical URLs and
structured business/gallery context.

That foundation works only when the content is specific. A page describing real wedding coverage
in Manila or corporate event work in Dubai gives a potential client and a search engine more to
understand than a generic slogan and an unlabeled image wall. Choose Studio Ninja for its
photographer-specific workflow; choose Gallurio when the searchable storefront and the operating
workspace need to be one product.

## Who this is not for

Stay with Studio Ninja if:

- Contracts and questionnaires are part of every wedding you book.
- Your workflow depends on automated stage reminders.
- You need clients to pay through a connected gateway.
- Photographer-specific job structure matters more to you than the price gap.
