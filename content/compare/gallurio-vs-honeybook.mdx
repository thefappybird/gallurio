## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if the work you need is a public portfolio connected to inquiries,
bookings, clients, calendars, and invoice PDFs at a lower regional price.

HoneyBook is a mature, broad platform for service businesses, with proposals, contracts,
e-signatures and in-app payment processing. Its United States Starter price is **$36 per month**
billed monthly or **$29 per month** billed annually. HoneyBook currently limits business-owner
availability to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.

Gallurio is a narrower tool: bookings, clients, a calendar, invoice PDFs, and a public portfolio
page with a contact form that turns an inquiry into a client and a booking in one step. It is
<GallurioPrice /> on a single plan.

If you need contracts and card processing inside one system, HoneyBook is the better fit and the
price gap is the cost of that. If you are paying $36 a month mostly to keep track of bookings and
send invoices, you are paying for a lot you are not using.

## What HoneyBook does that Gallurio does not

Worth stating plainly, because a comparison that only lists wins is not useful.

- **Proposals, contracts and e-signatures.** HoneyBook's core loop. Gallurio has none of it.
- **In-app payment processing.** HoneyBook takes the card. Gallurio issues the invoice and records
  that you were paid.
- **Automated workflows.** Scheduled email sequences, task automation, reminders. Gallurio sends
  transactional email and in-app notifications, but does not run sequences.
- **QuickBooks integration and scheduling links.** Both are HoneyBook features with no Gallurio
  equivalent.
- **An established user base in its supported countries**, which matters for templates, community
  answers and integrations.

## What Gallurio does that HoneyBook does not

- **A real creative storefront.** Independent Home and Gallery canvases can combine story,
  services, video, featured projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections with
  composable layouts, brand colours, and typography. HoneyBook has contact forms, not that public
  showcase.
- **One inquiry becomes a client and a booking.** The public form writes all three records in a
  single transaction, so nothing is half-created if something fails partway.
- **Team workspaces at no extra tier.** HoneyBook gates unlimited team members behind the $129
  plan.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate.** Gallurio bills the applicable USD tier and shows a
  converted headline when available. HoneyBook is USD only.
- **Five interface languages**, including Arabic with full right-to-left layout.

## Is Gallurio a HoneyBook alternative for portfolio discovery?

It can be when the missing part of the workflow is a public body of work, not contracts or payment
processing. Gallurio lets an owner configure the Home search title and description, a separate
Gallery description, social preview media, and whether the published site should be indexed. The
public pages use canonical URLs and structured business/gallery data so search engines and AI
retrieval systems have clearer context about the business.

That is a technical foundation, not a ranking guarantee. A useful portfolio still needs visible
copy naming the real service and location, representative projects, and a clear path to enquire.
HoneyBook remains the stronger choice when contracts, e-signatures, and card payments matter more
than owning a configurable public showcase.

## Who this is not for

Do not switch to Gallurio if any of these are true:

- You send contracts and need them signed inside the same tool.
- You want your clients to pay by card without leaving the invoice.
- Your business runs on automated email sequences.
- You need QuickBooks to reconcile automatically.

Those are real gaps, not roadmap items being downplayed.
