## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if you want a public portfolio, inquiries, bookings, clients, and invoice
PDFs in one lower-priced workspace without paying for bookkeeping or workflow automation.

17hats is one of the broadest small-business platforms in this category: quotes, contracts,
invoices, bookkeeping, scheduling and workflows in one subscription. List price is **$60 per
month**, discounted to about **$25 per month** when the standing annual promotion applies.

Gallurio covers bookings, clients, calendar, and invoices, then adds something 17hats does not have:
two configurable public canvases for story, services, video, featured projects, and gallery
collections. The same storefront creates the client and booking from its Contact experience. It is

## What 17hats does that Gallurio does not

- **Quotes and contracts with e-signatures.**
- **Bookkeeping** — income and expense tracking, tax categories.
- **Automated workflows** and scheduled client emails.
- **Online scheduling links** for consultations.
- **Payment processing** inside the invoice.

## What Gallurio does that 17hats does not

- **A creative storefront with two drag-and-drop canvases.** Home and Gallery can combine story,
  services, video, featured projects, grid or masonry galleries, and project collections using your
  own colours, typography, and layouts. This is the largest single difference between the products.
- **Inquiry to client to booking in one transaction**, so a submission never lands half-created.
- **Unlimited invoices on the paid plan**, against 17hats' free-tier cap of four per quarter.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate**, billed in USD and shown converted when a rate is available.
- **Five interface languages**, including right-to-left Arabic.

## Who this is not for

Stay on 17hats if:

- You need bookkeeping and your accountant already relies on it.
- Contracts and quotes are part of closing every job.
- You depend on scheduling links for consultations.
- The 50% promotion is active and $25 a month is not a number you are trying to reduce.
