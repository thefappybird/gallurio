## The short version

**Start with Gallurio** if you want one regional plan for the public portfolio, inquiry, booking,
client, calendar, team, and invoice workflow, while photo delivery already happens elsewhere.

Pixieset is the most serious competitor on this list, because it is not one product. Client Gallery
delivers photos. Website builds a site. Studio Manager runs bookings, contracts and invoices. There
is a genuine free tier, and the Suite bundles everything from around **$28 per month**.

Gallurio is one plan at <GallurioPrice />, with configurable Home and Gallery canvases for services,
video, featured projects, and gallery collections, plus bookings, clients, calendar, and invoices.
It does not deliver galleries in the sense Pixieset means — no proofing, favouriting, downloads, or
print store.

So the honest framing is not "which one wins." It is: if photo delivery is the centre of your
business, Pixieset is built for that and Gallurio is not.

galleries a year and not much else, free may beat a paid all-in-one plan.

## What Pixieset does that Gallurio does not

- **Client gallery delivery.** Proofing, favourites, download controls, expiry, password protection,
  a mobile gallery app. This is the product and Gallurio has no equivalent.
- **Print and product sales** with fulfilment, which for many photographers is revenue rather than
  convenience.
- **Contracts and e-signatures** in Studio Manager.
- **Payment processing** on invoices.
- **Photographer-specific structure** throughout — shoots, deliverables, galleries per session.

## What Gallurio does that Pixieset does not

- **Serves any event business**, not only photographers. Planners, caterers, venues and DJs use the
  same workspace, which matters if you do more than shoot.
- **A flexible storefront for the whole creative business.** Home and Gallery can mix services,
  video, individual images, featured projects, and collection-led grid or masonry layouts rather
  than treating the public site only as a delivery portal.
- **One inquiry becomes a client and a booking** in a single transaction from your public page.
- **A single plan with no product-by-product pricing.** Everything is included rather than assembled
  from Gallery plus Website plus Studio Manager.
- **Regional pricing with a local estimate.** Gallurio bills the applicable USD tier and shows a
  converted headline when available; Pixieset is USD.
- **Five interface languages**, including right-to-left Arabic.

## Who this is not for

Stay with Pixieset if:

- Gallery delivery is central to how you work and get paid.
- You sell prints or products through your galleries.
- The free tier already covers your volume.
- Contracts and card payments need to sit inside the same tool.

Running both is a reasonable answer and worth saying out loud: Pixieset for delivery, Gallurio for
the bookings behind it.
