## The short version

Most event businesses start in a spreadsheet, and that is the correct decision. It is free,
everyone knows how it works, and for the first twenty bookings it does the job.

It stops working at a predictable point, and the point is not "when you get big." It is when these
three overlap:

- **You need to send invoices.** A spreadsheet holds the numbers. It does not produce a branded PDF
  with line items, and copying figures by hand is where mistakes get expensive.
- **Someone else needs to edit it.** Two people in one sheet is fine. Two people plus a formula plus
  a filter view is how a column gets sorted independently of its neighbours and a whole row of data
  silently belongs to the wrong client.
- **You need history.** What did this client book in 2024, and what did they pay? A spreadsheet can
  answer that only if you never restructured it.

to free that the comparison is about your time rather than your budget.

## What a spreadsheet does that Gallurio does not

Being fair to the incumbent:

- **It costs nothing.**
- **It does anything.** Any column, any formula, any layout, changed in seconds.
- **Everyone already knows it.** No one needs training.
- **It exports and shares trivially**, and every accountant on earth accepts it.
- **It works offline** and will still open in ten years.

## What Gallurio does that a spreadsheet does not

- **Generates invoice PDFs** from a booking, with your branding.
- **Publishes a public portfolio** with home, gallery and contact pages.
- **Turns an enquiry into a client and a booking** in one step, with no re-typing.
- **Keeps client history automatically**, including what they paid.
- **Shows a real dashboard** — revenue over time, upcoming bookings, top clients — without you
  building the formulas.
- **Enforces structure**, so a booking cannot exist without a client and a row cannot be sorted into
  the wrong place.

## Who this is not for

Stay in the spreadsheet if:

- You take a handful of bookings a year.
- You invoice through your accountant and never touch it yourself.
- You are the only person who will ever open the file.
- Your process genuinely needs columns nobody else's business would have.
